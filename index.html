Review queue

Natural Workplace

I’m not completely sure this is the best approach, but I think it’s worth trialling first.

中文：我不完全确定这是最优方案，但我觉得值得先试一下。

Why it works: “worth trialling” sounds natural in NZ/AU workplace English and avoids sounding too direct.

Advanced Meeting

I’m not fully convinced it’s the optimal option yet, but we could test it and see how it goes.

中文：我还不完全相信这是最优选择，但我们可以测试一下看看效果。

Why it works: “not fully convinced” is professional and softer than saying “I disagree”.

Softer Team chat

It might not be perfect, but I’m happy to give it a go and review the outcome.

中文：它可能不是完美方案，但我愿意先试试，再看结果。

Why it works: “give it a go” is conversational and common in New Zealand.
